BITWORKS 20 t/s

BITWORKS

Build computers out of tiny blocks. Wires carry ON/OFF signals, gates do logic, LEDs show the result.

BUILDING

Left click/drag place block
Right click/drag erase
Shift+drag = straight line
Click a block of the same type = rotate it
R rotate (Shift+R back)
F change setting of block under mouse
  (clock speed, LED color, buzzer note)
Q pick the block under the mouse
1–0 quick select
Click a switch or button to use it

COPY + PASTE

Ctrl+drag = select area
Ctrl+C copy  Ctrl+X cut  Del delete
Ctrl+V paste (R rotates, click places)
Ctrl+Z undo  Ctrl+Y redo  Esc cancel

MOVING

WASD/arrows move (Shift = fast)
Middle mouse drag to move
Mouse wheel zoom
H go home to 0,0  G grid

SIMULATION

Space pause / run
. one tick at a time
[ ] slower / faster
M mute buzzers

HOW SIGNALS FLOW

Gates send out the front (the arrow).
AND/OR/XOR… read back + left + right.
NOT, DIODE and T flip-flop read only the back.
Switches, buttons and clocks power all 4 sides.
Every gate takes 1 tick.

Your world saves by itself. Press F1 or click anywhere to close.
